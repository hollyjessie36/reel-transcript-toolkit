# Subtitle review checklist

- Check names, numbers and negations against the original recording.
- Play each cut and listen for the start and end of the spoken phrase.
- Identical whole-second TXT labels can represent different cues. Compare their millisecond intervals before removing a duplicate.
- Retain the original SRT for timing; export TXT for reading or CSV for inspection.
- Preview the result in the intended player and check line breaks, overlap and timing.
