About Media to Words

Media to Words is an independent transcription tool, built and maintained by one developer. It helps you turn spoken words in a saved video or audio recording into text you can read, quote or subtitle.

What you can do here

Choose a local file to generate a timestamped transcript in your browser. Copy the words, save a TXT file or download SRT subtitles. Listen back before publishing, especially when a recording includes names or overlapping voices.

Contact contact@mediatowords.com.
