Privacy policy

Your recording stays on your device

When you choose Upload file, your browser reads the file and generates the transcript locally. We do not upload your recording or transcript. Closing or reloading the page clears the displayed result. Files you download remain on your device.

Network requests

Cloudflare serves this site and the model files. Your browser also downloads transcription software from jsDelivr. Like other web requests, these connections reveal your IP address to the provider serving the resource. Model files may be cached in your browser.

If you submit a public Instagram or TikTok link, Cloudflare routes it to our media service to retrieve the recording or its audio. The TikTok service uses temporary media files during processing and removes them afterward. Your browser generates the transcript and we do not keep a transcript history.

Usage counts

We also count download cancellations, timeouts and the processing stage reached. The relay aggregates media lookup outcomes and response-time ranges. We count starts, video retrieval outcomes, switches from a failed link to a file, transcription outcomes, copies and export clicks. Totals are grouped by day, tool page and input method. These counters store no IP addresses, identifiers, submitted URLs, filenames, recordings or transcript text. Browsers that signal Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track skip these counts.

Advertising

We plan to display third-party advertising in the future. This site currently has no ad scripts. Before ads are enabled, we will update this policy with the providers, their data practices and the choices available to you.

Contact

If you email us, your message and sender address reach our support inbox so we can reply. See our contact page.
