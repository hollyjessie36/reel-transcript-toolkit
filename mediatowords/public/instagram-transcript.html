TXT or SRT: which Instagram transcript file to download

Choose TXT when your next step is reading, searching or making notes. Each exported text line carries a start-time label, which is useful for locating the relevant passage. The Copy text action instead copies the words without those labels. Neither choice turns the text into a styled subtitle track in your editor.

Choose SRT when your next step is caption editing. An SRT export contains numbered cues, a start and end time for each cue, and the recognized or corrected text. Import it as a subtitle track alongside the same video version you transcribed. Preview the beginning, an edited section and the ending before publishing.

Keep both files if you need notes and subtitles. They serve different purposes within an instagram to text workflow. Correcting a word in the on-page editor changes the exported text but keeps the cue timing, so a timing problem needs a separate check in your editing workflow. Accuracy, languages and limits

The tool runs a speech-recognition model in the browser. Its language selector offers automatic detection and twelve named languages: English, Spanish, French, German, Indonesian, Portuguese, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, Hindi and Italian. Select the language spoken in the recording when you know it. The interface does not provide a translation mode, speaker-name assignment or a guaranteed accuracy score.

The file limit is 250 MiB, with one recording processed at a time. Browser decoding and available device memory also matter. A familiar file extension does not guarantee that the browser can read its audio track. If decoding fails, make a compatible audio export in your editor and try that recording instead.

Listen carefully to technical terms, names and numbers. Background music and overlapping speakers can cause incorrect or missing words. Instagram transcript FAQ

Can I get a transcript from a private Instagram account?

No. Private accounts and content that requires an Instagram login are not supported through the link input. This tool does not sign in to Instagram or bypass access restrictions. If you already have a video file you are authorized to process, choose Upload file and review it locally.

Does this work with Instagram Stories and video posts?

Public Reel links and single video post links are accepted. Stories, profiles and carousels are not supported link inputs. Even a public post can fail when Instagram blocks retrieval. Use a saved video you have permission to process if the link cannot be downloaded.

How accurate is the Instagram transcript?

Accuracy depends on the spoken language, recording quality, background sound and overlapping voices. There is no guaranteed accuracy percentage. Listen while reviewing the editable lines, check names and numbers, and compare the last spoken sentence with the last recognized line before downloading or quoting the transcript.

Which languages are supported?

The selector offers Auto-detect plus English, Spanish, French, German, Indonesian, Portuguese, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, Hindi and Italian. Choose the language actually spoken in the recording when possible. These options control speech recognition; they do not provide a promise of translation or equal accuracy across languages.

Can I transcribe an Instagram video without a link?

Yes. Choose Upload file and select a saved audio or video recording that your browser can decode, up to 250 MiB. Select its spoken language and press Transcribe file. The local recording stays on your device; the browser downloads the speech model on first use.

Can I download subtitles as SRT for editing?

Yes. After recognition, review the editable transcript lines and choose Download SRT. Your corrected text is included with numbered cues and start and end timestamps. Import that file into your editor as subtitles, then preview against the exact video export; text corrections do not automatically retime cues.

Is it legal to transcribe someone else's Reel?

Public visibility is not permission to reuse a recording or its words. Use content you own, have permission to process, or can use under an applicable legal exception. Republishing and sharing raise separate questions. Copyright rules depend on the circumstances and jurisdiction; seek permission when your rights are unclear.

Can I transcribe an Instagram video without subtitles?

Yes. The tool recognizes spoken audio rather than requiring an existing caption track. Use a supported public video link or an authorized local file, then review the generated text. Silent video or unreadable audio cannot provide a reliable spoken transcript.

Do I need to install an Instagram transcript generator app?
